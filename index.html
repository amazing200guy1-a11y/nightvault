How Your Privacy is Protected

1
ZK Commitment Creation
Your heir's identity and passphrase are hashed into a cryptographic commitment using SHA-256. The actual values never touch the blockchain.
2

3

When claiming, your heir proves knowledge of the passphrase via ZK proof. The chain verifies without ever learning the secret or your identity.
